## Notes on the asked-about areas

- **Tag parsing.** Sound apart from G-m1, G-m3 and G-M1. The hold-back of `<`, `<u` and `<ui-`,
  quoted `>` and `/>`, entity escapes, line breaks in values, duplicate attributes, the
  open-and-close form and the 20,000-character cap all behave as documented, and the specs
  cover them.
- **Schema checks.** The constructor refuses every unsupported keyword it should. `Object.hasOwn`
  guards `properties`, element ids and view columns. The gaps are G-m1 and G-n3.
- **Document structure.** One parent per element, cycles, depth and element caps are right, and
  the cycle walk is linear. The only gap is the partial case, G-m2.
- **Artifact registry.** Loads de-duplicate by range, a failed range loads again, and `clear()`
  and `destroy()` abort loads in flight without writing late results. The gap is G-m4.
- **Actions.** `openUrl` allows only `http` and `https` origins on the list, and none by default.
  `send` caps text at 200 and context at 2,000 characters. The context itself is G-M2.
- **React.** Hooks order is stable in `chatUiComponent` and the boundaries, keys are stable as
  segments grow, and the grid and chart keep one model while props stream. Re-render cost is
  G-m7.
- **Tool groups.** Every Hoist API used checks out: `setSortBy` with `GridSorter` strings,
  `setGroupBy`, `updateColumnState`, `setColumnState` (which drops duplicate ids),
  `StoreSelectionModel.select`, `FieldFilterSpec.valueType`, `addView`, `loadState` and
  `onRglLayoutChange`. Each undo restores what its tool changed. The gap is the locks, G-m8.

## Keep

- The catalog design: one document model for four delivery modes, schema-checked props with
  readable paths, and fallbacks per element.
- The tag parser's two-step design: a quote-aware scan to find the tag, then the XML parser on an
  escaped copy. No regular expression reads attribute values.
- Data by reference through a registry with shared, cached range loads and synthetic row ids.
- The helper tool groups: schemas from getters that follow the model, structure-only context, and
  undo from Hoist state. The specs run against real `GridModel` and `DashCanvasModel` instances.
- Citation markers as a remark plugin on the parsed tree, so code, links and footnotes stay as
  written.
