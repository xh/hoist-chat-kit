# Review 2 - Components

Scope: `desktop/cmp/chat/**`, `cmp/markdown/**`, `styles/**`, reviewed at HEAD `250c6c3`. Line
numbers refer to HEAD. Uncommitted work in the tree at review time (`display: 'inline'` in
`ChatToolCall`, a third `chatModel` argument on `ChatDataRenderer`, `genui/`) is noted where it
bears on a finding. It was not reviewed in full.

## Keep

- The stick-to-bottom design: a content `ResizeObserver` that follows only while busy, a wheel-up escape, the re-stick threshold, and a jump to the bottom on send.
- Immutable parts with memoized `textPart` and `chatReasoning`. Text parts that are not streaming really do skip render.
- `closeMarkdownSyntax`, with good specs, and fence-aware block splitting. Finished blocks are not re-parsed while streaming.
- Consistent component specs: `withFactory`, `displayName`, `className` in the spec, local models named `impl` with `xhImpl = true`, and `model: false` where there is no model.
- Hoist `button`, `textArea` and `clipboardButton` throughout. The one raw `input type=file` is justified, as Hoist's `FileChooser` is too heavy for this use.
- The theme variables follow Hoist's `--xh-x: var(--x, default)` pattern, with dark overrides under `body.xh-dark`, and no hard-coded colors outside `vars.scss`. Overrides set `--xh-button-*` variables rather than colors.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` handling, `role="alert"` on the error banner, `aria-expanded` on the disclosures, and an IME check that covers Safari's `keyCode 229`.
- An edit flow that the list controls, so the error banner's Edit and the message's Edit share one state.
- Clean comments: no em dashes and no history.
