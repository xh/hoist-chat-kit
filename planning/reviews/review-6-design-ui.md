## Notes on the asked-about areas

- **Busy, stall and countdown.** Stall timers and the composer's timers and listeners clear on
  destroy. `syncRetryWait` resets when the last message changes. `refreshStatusAsync` detaches a
  stalled turn before it reloads the thread. The gaps are retry-safety (D-M1), the cap and owner
  of the auto-retry (D-m5, D-m6) and paused time (D-m4).
- **Drafts.** The draft moves correctly when a chat adopts a backend thread id mid-turn. The gaps
  are D-M2, D-M8 and D-m2.
- **Side panel.** The workspace closes a request when the thread changes, and keys each view by
  request. The gap is the silent document click (D-m13).
- **Timeline.** Grouping by display follows D78. The gaps are settling: sub-agents (D-M3), stopped
  calls (D-M4) and paused turns (D-m14).
- **Announcer.** The single-host rule holds while one list stays mounted. The gaps are what happens
  as lists come and go (D-M5, D-M6, D-m7) and components with regions of their own (D-m9).
- **Keyboard.** `/` does nothing inside a text field. A shortcut an inner component handled does
  not fire again. Escape in the feedback form, the edit box, the hover card and the timeline
  details calls `preventDefault`, and the list honors it. The gaps are focus after actions
  (D-m10) and the Mac delete key (D-n12).
- **Layering.** No platform-neutral package imports `desktop/`. Every new public promise method has
  the `Async` suffix. The D57 gaps are D-M7, D-m1 and D-n1, and `ChatThreadList` takes its host as
  `model` while the list and composer take `host`, a naming split to weigh.
- **Leaks.** Workspace and helper ResizeObservers disconnect through `useComposedRefs`. Chart
  models are `@managed`. The hover card and `useChatClock` clear their timers. The gaps are D-M3's
  ticking row, D-n2 and D-n3.
- **CSS.** The convention holds throughout (see Check results). The gap is dead tokens (D-m21).
